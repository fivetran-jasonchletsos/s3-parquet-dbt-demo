Andy asked whether dbt can transform parquet files stored in S3 — it can, because dbt compiles SQL and the engine reads the parquet in place with no load into a warehouse. I ran identical dbt models three ways (DuckDB local, DuckDB reading s3:// directly, Snowflake external tables) and all three went 15/15 with exact parity: fct_daily_revenue = 3,005 rows, gross revenue 735,258.78. Same pattern Fivetran MDLS enables, with connector data landed in S3 as parquet-backed Iceberg. Repo: github.com/fivetran-jasonchletsos/s3-parquet-dbt-demo. Write-up: fivetran-jasonchletsos.github.io/s3-parquet-dbt-demo.
